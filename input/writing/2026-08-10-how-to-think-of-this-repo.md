This repo serves its purpose as a living document demonstrating its princples more than an abstract generalized system. By the nature of the rules themselves, stripping it down would really only give another user a bare directory structure. No code, no docs, no prose, literally nothing - just a series of directories and a document (notably not authored by them) detailing the principles and techniques of the system. As such, whether this repo is ever even deserving of a generalized clonable repo seed is kinda up in the air. The fact that its use as a generalized technology is up for debate may be a strength, in that it keeps expression and design honest and prevents premature optimization, as the only gains really look to be in the usage of the repo itself.
